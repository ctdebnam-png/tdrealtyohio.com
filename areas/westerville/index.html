Buying or selling a home in Westerville

TD Realty Ohio is based in Westerville, so this is the brokerage's home market. The city covers about 12.6 square miles on the northeast side of the Columbus area and is home to Otterbein University and the shops and restaurants of Uptown Westerville along State Street.

Around Westerville

Westerville grew around Otterbein, which became a college in 1847, and its center is still the campus and Uptown. The Hanby House, home of Benjamin Hanby, who wrote "Up On The Housetop," is on the National Register of Historic Places and is run as a museum. The Anti-Saloon League moved its national headquarters to Westerville in 1909, and that building is now a museum attached to the public library. The city lies in the land between Alum Creek and Big Walnut Creek.

Selling in Westerville

Westerville sits on the line between Franklin and Delaware counties, and that shapes a sale more than most sellers expect. The county decides which auditor holds the property record, which tax bill is prorated at closing, and where the deed is recorded. Travis confirms those details before the home goes live, so the listing, the purchase contract and the title work all describe the same property.

Homes here range from older houses near Uptown and the Otterbein campus to subdivisions built further out from the center. An older house raises questions a newer one does not: original systems, past additions, and for homes built before 1978, the federal lead-based paint disclosure. Ohio also requires most sellers to give the buyer a completed residential property disclosure form before accepting an offer. TD Realty Ohio works through both before launch, so the buyer learns about the house from the seller and not from the inspector.

Buying in Westerville

A Westerville mailing address does not always mean the City of Westerville. Some addresses with that name sit in neighboring townships or in Columbus, and the school district, municipal services and income tax follow the parcel, not the post office. Before you write an offer, Travis checks the county auditor record for the home so you know which city, township and school district you are buying into.

Interstate access is often the first filter here, since the city borders both I-71 and I-270. From there the search narrows by budget, the type of home and how much work you are willing to take on. Travis tours with you, pulls nearby comparable sales before you set a price, and stays on the file through inspection, appraisal and closing.
