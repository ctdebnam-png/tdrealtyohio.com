About Westerville

Westerville combines small-town charm with suburban convenience. The historic Uptown district offers local shops and restaurants, while newer developments provide modern housing options. Westerville City Schools consistently ranks among Ohio's top districts. The city is home to Otterbein University and offers extensive parks and recreation programs.

Living in Westerville

Schools: Westerville City Schools. Ranked in the top 10 statewide with strong academics across all levels.

Commute: 15 minutes to downtown Columbus via I-270.

Lifestyle: Walkable Uptown district with local shops, restaurants, and community events. Over 50 parks and extensive trail system. Home to Otterbein University, adding cultural programming and campus green space.

Market Context: Homes typically range from $300K to $600K. The area appeals to families seeking top-ranked schools, ample parks, and a strong sense of community.

Frequently Asked Questions About Westerville Real Estate

Is Westerville a good place to raise a family?
Westerville is consistently rated one of Ohio's best family communities. The school district ranks in the top 10 statewide, and the city offers over 50 parks, a community center, and numerous youth sports programs.
What is the Westerville housing market like?
Westerville homes typically sell within 12 days. The market is competitive with strong demand from families drawn to the school district. Homes range from $250K condos to $800K+ estates.
What are Westerville's most popular neighborhoods?
Heritage District near Uptown is prized for walkability. Blendon Chase and Spring Creek offer newer construction. Huber Village and Hometowne areas provide more affordable options.
