Buying or selling a home in Dublin

Dublin sits on both banks of the Scioto River on the northwest side of the Columbus area. Its oldest blocks, Historic Dublin, grew from John Sells's original land purchase, and the city hosts the Dublin Irish Festival each summer.

Around Dublin

Several of Dublin's parks follow the Scioto River, including the two Dublin Kiwanis Riverway parks. Scioto Park holds the Leatherlips sculpture, created in 1990 to honor Chief Shateyaronyah, one of many works in the city's Art in Public Places collection. Glacier Ridge Metro Park, on the city's edge and run by Metro Parks, has trails, disc golf and horseback riding. Muirfield Village Golf Club and its surrounding neighborhood are on the north side.

Selling in Dublin

Few cities in Ohio cross four county lines, and Dublin does. For a seller that means the basic facts in a listing, including the county, the tax figures and the school district, have to come from the parcel record and not from memory or a neighbor's listing. Travis pulls the auditor record for your home before launch, and the listing states only what that record shows.

If your home is in one of Dublin's subdivisions with a homeowners association, buyers will ask early about its rules, dues and any approvals for past exterior changes. Have those documents ready. Buyers also ask how a home connects to the city's trails and parks along the Scioto, and a listing that answers that plainly, with the name of the trail or park, is more useful than one that only lists the rooms.

Buying in Dublin

Two homes a mile apart in Dublin can sit in different counties and, in some areas, different school districts. Before you write an offer, Travis confirms the county, the district and the association for that address, and reads the association documents with you so the rules and costs are clear before you are under contract.

Commute is usually the next question. I-270, State Route 161 and U.S. Route 33 all run through the city, and the right part of Dublin depends on where you are driving each day. Travis tours with you, gathers comparable sales before you set your price, and manages inspection, appraisal and closing.
