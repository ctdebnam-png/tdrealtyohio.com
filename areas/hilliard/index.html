Buying or selling a home in Hilliard

Hilliard is a Franklin County city in Norwich Township on the west side of the Columbus area. Its historic district runs along Norwich Street in Old Hilliard, where the restored train station stands in Weaver Park and the Heritage Rail Trail begins.

Around Hilliard

Old Hilliard keeps the town's original layout along Norwich Street, with a historic district of older homes and the restored train station in Weaver Park. The town began as Hilliard's Station on the Piqua and Indiana Railroad, and the Heritage Rail Trail now starts at Center Street in Old Hilliard. The Hilliard Historical Society keeps a historical village near the Franklin County Fairgrounds, which host the Franklin County Fair each year. The city also holds the Old Hilliardfest Art and Street Fair and a Fourth of July parade and fireworks.

Selling in Hilliard

The Hilliard City School District covers a portion of Columbus about the same size as the part inside Hilliard. Many homes in the district have a Columbus or township address, and city, township and school district each affect taxes and services. Travis takes the city, township and school district for your listing from the county auditor record so the listing is accurate from the first day.

Hilliard grew quickly after I-270 was completed in the early 1970s, so many homes date from the decades since, alongside older houses near Old Hilliard. Pricing compares your home with similar homes of the same era nearby. Travis prepares that comparison, recommends which repairs and updates are worth doing before launch, and completes Ohio's required property disclosure form with you.
