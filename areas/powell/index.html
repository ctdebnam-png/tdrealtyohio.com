Buying or selling a home in Powell

Powell is a Delaware County city in Liberty Township, between the Scioto and Olentangy rivers. Its historic downtown centers on the crossing of State Route 750 and Liberty Street, and Village Green Park hosts the annual Powell Festival.

Around Powell

Powell sits in Liberty Township, the site of the first settlement in Delaware County, and its downtown has its own Historic Downtown Advisory Commission. The city's parks are central to its calendar. Village Green Park, home of the Powell Festival, has a splash pad, a concert amphitheater, a playground and pavilion shelters. Other parks include Arbor Ridge, Oak, Beechwood, Meadowview, Murphy and Seldom Seen, with courts for basketball, volleyball, tennis and pickleball and fields for soccer. Bike paths and multiuse trails connect many of the parks to the subdivisions around them.

Selling in Powell

Powell is a small city inside Liberty Township, and a Powell mailing address can sit in the township rather than the city. Taxes, zoning and some services depend on which one your parcel is in. Travis confirms it from the Delaware County Auditor record before your listing goes live, and the listing states what the record says.

If your home is in a subdivision with a homeowners association, buyers will ask for the association's rules, dues and any approvals for past exterior changes, so gather them early. Buying in Powell

For a buyer the key checks in Powell are the same ones that matter for a seller: city or township, school district, and association. Travis confirms all three from the auditor record and the association documents before you are under contract, so the monthly costs and the rules are clear.

Powell is about 15 miles from downtown Columbus in a straight line, and the commute is worth testing at the hours you would actually drive it.
