Buying or selling a home in Delaware

Delaware is the seat of Delaware County, founded in 1808 and home to Ohio Wesleyan University since 1844. The Olentangy River runs through the city, and its Historic Northwest Neighborhood holds more than 500 homes and carriage houses listed on the National Register of Historic Places.

Around Delaware

Downtown Delaware sits along the Olentangy River beside the Ohio Wesleyan campus, and the city's daily paper, The Delaware Gazette, has published since 1818. The Delaware County Fair, held at the city's fairgrounds, includes the Little Brown Jug harness race. The Historic Northwest Neighborhood holds more than 500 homes and carriage houses on the National Register. Boardman Arts Park and the Delaware Arts Festival are part of the city's arts calendar.

Selling in Delaware

Delaware has many older homes, especially in the Historic Northwest Neighborhood near downtown. National Register listing places no federal restrictions on a private owner, but buyers will ask about it, and older houses raise their own questions: original systems, past additions, and the federal lead-based paint disclosure for homes built before 1978. Travis works through those with you before the home goes on the market.

The city also has newer subdivisions, and those homes are priced against a different set of sales. TD Realty Ohio sets your price from comparable homes of similar age and location, recommends the repairs worth doing, completes Ohio's residential property disclosure form with you, and coordinates showings, offers and closing.

The full listing process is on the seller representation page.

Buying in Delaware

Delaware is far enough from Columbus that the drive on U.S. Route 23 shapes most decisions, so test it at the hours you would drive it. Travis helps you set the search around commute, budget and the kind of home you want, whether that is a historic house near downtown or newer construction at the edges of the city.

In older homes, a careful inspection matters more than usual, including a camera inspection of the sewer line. Travis helps you decide what to raise with the seller, confirms the parcel's city, township and school district on the Delaware County Auditor record, prices your offer from comparable sales, and manages appraisal and closing.
