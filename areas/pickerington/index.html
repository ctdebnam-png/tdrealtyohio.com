Buying or selling a home in Pickerington

Pickerington was founded in 1815 as Jacksonville and renamed in 1827 for its founder, Abraham Pickering. The Ohio legislature named it the Violet Capital of Ohio in 1996, and the Motorcycle Hall of Fame sits just off Interstate 70 at the north edge of the city.

Around Pickerington

Pickerington was certified as a city in 1991. Olde Pickerington Village is its historic center, reached by Hill Road from Reynoldsburg to the north. Pickerington's parks department maintains more than 158 acres with a community pool, shelter houses, fishing ponds and ball fields, and Pickerington Ponds Metro Park is part of the Metro Parks system. The Pickerington Local School District runs two high schools, two junior high schools, three middle schools and seven elementary schools, along with an alternative high school and an early childhood center.

Selling in Pickerington

Pickerington is the second-largest city in Fairfield County, and part of it lies in Franklin County, so the county record is the first thing to confirm. It sets the tax figures in your listing, the auditor that holds your parcel and where the deed is recorded. Travis pulls the record before launch and the listing states only what it shows.

Housing ranges from homes near Olde Pickerington Village to subdivisions built as the city grew outward. If your neighborhood has a homeowners association, have its rules and dues ready for buyers. TD Realty Ohio prices your home from sales of similar homes nearby, plans prep and launch, and completes Ohio's required property disclosure form with you before offers arrive.

Buying in Pickerington

School district lines and city limits do not match exactly here, as in most of Central Ohio, and a Pickerington mailing address can sit outside the city. Travis confirms the city or township, the county and the school district for every home you consider before you make an offer.

Interstate 70 is the main route to downtown Columbus and the east side, so the distance from your home to the highway matters on a daily basis. Travis helps you weigh that, tours with you, prices your offer from comparable sales, and manages inspection, appraisal and closing.
