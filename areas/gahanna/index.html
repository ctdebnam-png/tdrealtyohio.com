Buying or selling a home in Gahanna

Gahanna was founded in 1849 along Big Walnut Creek in northeastern Franklin County. The name comes from a Native American word for three creeks joining into one, and the creek still runs through the center of the city at Creekside and Olde Gahanna.

Around Gahanna

Gahanna has invested in its old center along the creek. The Creekside redevelopment, begun in 2004, added public spaces, restaurants, residences and offices next to the park along Big Walnut Creek, and Olde Gahanna has been revitalized around it. Central Park of Gahanna occupies a 200-acre site that includes a golf academy. U.S. Route 62 connects to I-670 for direct road access to the airport, and State Route 317 is the main north-south road through downtown.

Selling in Gahanna

Big Walnut Creek and its tributaries, Rocky Fork Creek and Sycamore Run, cross much of Gahanna. If your home sits near one of them, look up its flood zone on the FEMA flood map before you list. A buyer's lender will check it, and a seller who already knows the answer, and has any elevation certificate or flood insurance history on hand, keeps that question from stalling the contract.

Gahanna's housing includes older homes near Olde Gahanna and later subdivisions across the rest of the city. Pricing should compare your home to sales of similar age and size nearby rather than a citywide figure. Travis prepares that comparison, recommends which repairs matter before launch, and handles Ohio's required property disclosure form with you so it is complete before offers arrive.

Buying in Gahanna

If you travel often, Gahanna's position next to the airport and between I-270 and I-670 is usually the reason it is on your list. Travis helps you weigh that access against the other things you need from the home, sets up tours, and gathers comparable sales so your offer price rests on recent sales and not on the asking price.

Two checks are worth doing on any Gahanna home before you commit: the flood zone, for homes near the creeks, and the county auditor record, which confirms the city or township and the school district for the parcel. A Gahanna mailing address can be outside the city. Travis runs both checks and then manages inspection, appraisal and closing.
