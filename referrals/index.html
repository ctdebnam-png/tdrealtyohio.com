Referral Program

Referred by a Friend? Get $250 at Closing.

Our way of saying thanks for trusting us.

Claim Your Referral Credit

If a friend, family member, or colleague sent you our way, let us know. When you close on your purchase or sale with TD Realty Ohio, you'll receive a $250 credit at closing. Just tell us who referred you.

How it works: Complete the form with your information and the name of the person who referred you. Once your transaction closes, we'll apply the $250 credit to your closing costs.

Referral Credit Form

Referral Program Terms:
  • $250 credit applied at closing when referral completes a transaction
  • Referral must be a new client not currently working with TD Realty Ohio
  • Referrer must be identified before showing agreement is signed
  • One credit per referred transaction; paid within 30 days of closing
