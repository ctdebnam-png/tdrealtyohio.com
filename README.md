## Business model used sitewide
### Consumer-facing
TD Realty Ohio is a standard full-service real estate brokerage serving Central Ohio with direct broker access for buying, selling, and leasing.

### Agent-facing
- 100% on listing sides
- 90/10 split on buy sides
- 25% of the first month’s lease on rental transactions
