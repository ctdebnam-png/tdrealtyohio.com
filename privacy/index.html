Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Information We Collect

TD Realty Ohio, LLC ("we," "our," or "us") collects information you provide directly to us, such as when you:

  • Fill out a contact form on our website
  • Request information about our services
  • Engage us as your real estate agent
  • Communicate with us via phone, email, or other methods

This information may include your name, email address, phone number, property address, and other details relevant to your real estate transaction.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide requested services
  • Facilitate real estate transactions on your behalf
  • Send you information about properties or market updates (with your consent)
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Improve our services and website

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Other parties to a real estate transaction (buyers, sellers, other agents) as necessary
  • Service providers who assist with our business operations (see below)
  • Government agencies as required by law
  • MLS systems as required for listing properties

Services We Use

This website relies on the following third-party services to operate:

  • Hosting: Cloudflare Pages — serves this website and processes page requests
  • Form handling: Formspree — receives contact form submissions and forwards them to us via email
  • Analytics: Google Analytics (via Google Tag Manager) — collects anonymous usage data such as pages visited, time on site, and referral source. No personal information is shared with Google for advertising.
  • Fonts: Google Fonts — loads typefaces used on this site

Each service has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data.

Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained for up to 24 months to support ongoing client conversations and transaction records. Analytics data is retained in aggregated form. We delete personal information when it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time. To make a request, email info@tdrealtyohio.com with the subject line "Privacy Request." We will respond within 30 days.
