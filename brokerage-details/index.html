Fee Structure

TD Realty Ohio charges a 1% listing commission when the seller also buys their next home through the brokerage, or a 2% listing commission for sell-only transactions.

Buyer agent compensation is separate and negotiated on a per-transaction basis.

First-time homebuyers working with TD Realty Ohio receive 1% cash back at closing, which can be applied toward closing costs, rate buy-downs, or move-in expenses. Cash back is subject to lender approval.
