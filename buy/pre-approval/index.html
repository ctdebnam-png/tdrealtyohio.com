Get Pre-Approved to Buy Your Home

A mortgage pre-approval is the first step toward a confident, competitive home purchase. Let us connect you with trusted Central Ohio lenders.

Why Pre-Approval Matters

Before you start touring homes, a mortgage pre-approval gives you a clear picture of what you can afford and shows sellers that you are a serious, qualified buyer. In the competitive Central Ohio market, pre-approval is not optional — it is essential.

Know Your Budget

Stop guessing. A pre-approval letter tells you exactly how much home you can afford, so you can focus your search on properties that fit your financial situation.

Make Stronger Offers

Sellers take pre-approved buyers more seriously. When multiple offers come in, a buyer with pre-approval in hand has a significant advantage over one without.

Close Faster

Much of the mortgage paperwork is completed during pre-approval. That means once your offer is accepted, you can move to closing more quickly and with fewer surprises.

How TD Realty Ohio Helps

We work with a network of local lenders throughout Central Ohio who offer competitive rates and responsive service. When you fill out the form below, we will learn about your situation and connect you with a lender who is a good match for your needs. There is no cost and no obligation.

What you will need for pre-approval

  • Proof of income (pay stubs, W-2s, or tax returns)
  • Bank and investment account statements
  • Photo identification
  • Social Security number for the credit check
  • Employment verification details

Do not worry if you do not have everything gathered yet. Our lender partners will walk you through the process step by step.
