Claim Your 1% Cash Back at Closing

First-time home buyers in Central Ohio can receive 1% of the purchase price back at closing when they buy with TD Realty Ohio.

How the 1% Cash Back Program Works

Buying your first home is one of the biggest financial milestones you will reach. TD Realty Ohio wants to make that milestone even more rewarding. When you purchase your first home with us, we credit 1% of the purchase price back to you at closing. It is that straightforward.

Here is what 1% looks like on real numbers

$300,000 Home $3,000 Cash Back
$350,000 Home $3,500 Cash Back
$400,000 Home $4,000 Cash Back

That money can go toward closing costs, moving expenses, new furniture, or anything else you need as you settle into your home.

Program Details

  • Available to first-time home buyers purchasing in Central Ohio
  • The 1% cash back is credited at closing from our brokerage commission
  • Subject to lender approval — your lender must permit the credit
  • Cannot be combined with other commission rebate offers
  • You must be represented by TD Realty Ohio as your buyer’s agent from the start of your home search
