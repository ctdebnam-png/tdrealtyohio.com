Free Buyer Consultation

Not sure where to start? Sit down with a TD Realty Ohio agent and get personalized guidance before you begin your home search.

What Your Free Consultation Covers

Buying a home involves dozens of decisions, and you do not have to figure them all out on your own. Our no-pressure buyer consultation is designed to give you clarity, confidence, and a clear path forward — whether you are ready to start looking next week or six months from now.

Market Overview

We will walk you through current conditions in the Central Ohio housing market — what homes are selling for, how fast they are moving, and what that means for your budget and negotiating strategy.

Neighborhood Guidance

From Westerville to Dublin to Gahanna and everywhere in between, we will help you narrow down the neighborhoods that match your lifestyle, commute, school preferences, and budget.

Timeline Planning

We will map out a realistic timeline from today through closing day. Whether you need to sell a home first, wait for a lease to end, or are ready now, we will build a plan that works for your schedule.

Lender Connections

If you have not yet been pre-approved, we will connect you with trusted local lenders who can help you understand your borrowing power and lock in competitive rates.

Why schedule a consultation?

  • It is completely free with no obligation
  • You will leave with a clear understanding of your next steps
  • We can meet in person, by phone, or over video — whatever works for you
  • You get honest, local expertise without any sales pressure
