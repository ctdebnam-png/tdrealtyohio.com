# Community Events Template

This template is used by `scripts/content_generator.py` to generate weekly events roundup posts.

## Variables

- `{community_name}` - Display name of the community
- `{week_start_date}` - Start date of the week covered

## Structure

1. Introduction about the community this week
2. Upcoming events list (dates, times, locations)
3. Community news summary
4. "Why {community_name}?" highlight section
5. Subtle TD Realty Ohio mention

## Data Sources

- City event calendars (RSS feeds)
- Columbus Underground
- Local news RSS feeds

## SEO Requirements

- Title: "What's Happening in {community} - Week of {date}"
- Meta description: 150-160 chars
- Internal link to /areas/
- Minimum 600 words
