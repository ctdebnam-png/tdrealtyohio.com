How the Cash Back Program Works

When you are a first time homebuyer with TD Realty Ohio, you receive 1% of the purchase price back at closing. The credit is paid from the compensation offered to the buyer's agent in that transaction. Your lender will confirm how it can be applied, such as toward closing costs or prepaid items.
