How We Get Paid

Real estate fees can be confusing. Here's a plain explanation.

The Listing Fee (What Sellers Pay)

When you hire TD Realty Ohio to sell your home, you pay a listing fee.

  • 1% of the sale price if you also buy your next home through us.
  • 2% of the sale price if you're only selling.

This covers: MLS listing, professional photography, pricing strategy, showing coordination, negotiation, and contract-to-close management.

Every listing includes a free pre-listing home inspection ($400-500 value).

Buyer Agent Compensation (Separate)

If a buyer has their own agent, that agent's compensation is negotiated separately as part of the transaction.

This is standard in Ohio real estate and is not set or controlled by TD Realty Ohio.

As a seller, you'll see exactly what's being offered before you agree.

What First-Time Buyers Get

First-time homebuyers who work with TD Realty Ohio receive 1% of the purchase price back at closing.

On a $300,000 home, that's $3,000 toward closing costs or rate buydown.

Subject to lender approval.

No Hidden Fees

No desk fees, no transaction fees, no administrative charges.

All terms are in writing before you commit.
