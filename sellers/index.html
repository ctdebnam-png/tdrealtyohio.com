For Central Ohio Home Sellers

Sell with a plan, not guesswork.

From pricing through closing, TD Realty Ohio keeps your listing process organized and decision-ready. The focus is practical execution: market positioning, launch quality, showing strategy, offer evaluation, and timeline management.

Your listing process, step by step

Pricing strategy

We review comparable sales, active competition, and current pace of demand to establish a list strategy grounded in local conditions.

Prep priorities

Before launch, we identify improvements that matter most, recommend what can be skipped, and align the property for strong first impressions.

Launch and marketing execution

Your launch plan covers listing presentation, online exposure, and showing readiness so buyers see clear value from day one.

Showing and feedback management

We monitor showing activity and buyer feedback to decide if pricing, presentation, or terms should be adjusted.

Offer review and risk evaluation

Price is one part of the decision. We compare financing strength, contingencies, timelines, and net proceeds before negotiating terms.

Contract-to-close coordination

Once under contract, we track deadlines and communication across buyers, lenders, title, and vendors to keep the transaction moving cleanly.

Seller FAQ

How is my list price determined?

We analyze comparable sales, current inventory, and buyer demand in your price range to set a strategy that balances attention and value.

What should I fix before listing?

We prioritize improvements that affect buyer confidence and marketability, while avoiding unnecessary spending on low-impact updates.

How are offers compared?

We review net proceeds, financing reliability, contingencies, timeline fit, and risk exposure so you can evaluate each offer with clarity.

What happens after I accept an offer?

We guide you through inspections, appraisal, title work, and closing logistics while keeping deadlines and communication organized.
