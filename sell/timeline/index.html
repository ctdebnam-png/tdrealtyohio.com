Plan Your Selling Timeline

Selling one home while buying another takes careful coordination. Should you sell first to know exactly what you can afford, or buy first so you don't end up between homes? Can you close both the same week? Each approach has trade-offs around financing, contingencies, and logistics.

Tell us where you are in the process and we'll map out a realistic timeline — including prep time, expected days on market, inspection and appraisal windows, and closing coordination. We handle both sides of the transaction so the timing works for you, not against you.

What's your preferred approach?

We'll review your situation and reach out within one business day to discuss timing options. No obligation, no pressure.
