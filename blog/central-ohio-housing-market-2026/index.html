Central Ohio Housing Market Update: January 2026

By Travis Debnam | January 2026 | 9 min read

Here's what shifted in the Central Ohio housing market this January and what it means if you're buying or selling in the Columbus metro area.

What Changed This Month

Mortgage rates stabilized in the low-to-mid 6% range after months of fluctuation. That steadiness has brought more buyers off the sidelines, particularly in the $250K–$450K range where most Central Ohio transactions happen.

Inventory remains tight. New listings in January came in below the 5-year average for the Columbus metro, which continues to favor sellers in most price brackets. However, homes priced more than 5% above recent comparable sales are sitting longer — buyers are informed and selective.

Several Central Ohio communities are seeing particular activity:

  • Westerville — Strong demand in the Uptown district; homes under $400K moving within 2 weeks
  • Dublin — Bridge Park area continues to drive interest; corporate relocation activity is steady
  • Powell — Olentangy school district keeps family demand consistent
  • Clintonville and Grandview Heights — Limited inventory keeping prices firm
  • Gahanna and Hilliard — More affordable entry points attracting first-time buyers

What Buyers Should Do

  1. Get pre-approved before touring. In a market where good homes move fast, a pre-approval letter separates serious offers from casual ones.
  2. Focus on neighborhoods with room to grow. Gahanna, Grove City, and Reynoldsburg offer lower entry points with solid fundamentals. See all areas we serve.
  3. Don't waive the inspection. Even in competitive situations, an inspection protects your investment. A few hundred dollars now can save thousands later.
  4. Understand your total cost. Use our affordability calculator to see what you can realistically budget.
  5. Ask about cash back. First-time buyers working with TD Realty Ohio receive 1% of the purchase price back at closing.

What Sellers Should Do

  1. Price from comparable sales, not hope. Overpriced homes sit and accumulate days on market, which makes buyers suspicious. Accurate pricing from day one generates the most interest.
  2. Get a pre-listing inspection before you list. Knowing your home's condition lets you price accurately, make targeted repairs, and avoid surprises during buyer negotiations. TD Realty Ohio includes this free with every listing.
  3. Invest in presentation. Professional photography and a clean, decluttered home are non-negotiable. Most buyers find homes online first.
  4. Understand your listing fee options. A traditional 3% listing fee on a $350,000 home is $10,500. At 1% with TD Realty Ohio (sell + buy), that's $3,500 — keeping $7,000 more in your pocket.
  5. Evaluate offers holistically. Price matters, but so do contingencies, closing timeline, and financing strength. A slightly lower offer with clean terms can be the better deal.
