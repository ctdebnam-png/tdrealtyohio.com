How buying support works

  1. Discovery call outputs: budget guardrails, location priorities, property constraints, and timeline documented once and refined as homes are reviewed.
  2. Tour-to-offer workflow: each property is evaluated against your criteria with updated ranking notes after every tour.
  3. Offer strategy mechanics: offer structure options, escalation terms when appropriate, appraisal-gap risk review, and inspection strategy tradeoffs.
  4. Inspection response approach: request categories are prioritized by safety, function, and likely lender impact.
  5. Contract-to-close management: deadline tracking, lender/title coordination, inspection response timing, and final walkthrough checklist.
