Buying a home is more than finding listings. TD Realty Ohio helps you build a plan, evaluate homes with context, write competitive offers, and stay on track through inspections, appraisal, financing, and closing.

How we work with buyers

1) Plan and prepare

We start with your budget, target areas, timeline, and goals. You get practical guidance on financing readiness, monthly payment comfort, and what to handle first.

2) Search with a strategy

We focus your home search around lifestyle fit and resale considerations, then build a smart tour schedule so you can compare options without decision fatigue.

3) Write and negotiate

When you are ready to offer, we review price, terms, contingencies, and risk points so your offer is competitive while still protecting your position.

4) Manage contract milestones

From acceptance to closing, we coordinate the key milestones: inspections, repair negotiations, appraisal, financing deadlines, and final walkthrough.

Buyer FAQ

When should I talk to an agent?

As early as possible. Planning before you actively tour homes helps you avoid rushed decisions and keeps your timeline realistic.

Do I need a pre-approval before showings?

It is strongly recommended. A pre-approval clarifies budget and makes it easier to move quickly if the right property comes up.

How do I stay competitive without overpaying?

We evaluate current market conditions, recent comparable sales, and seller priorities to structure terms that are competitive and intentional.

What happens if inspection finds issues?

We walk through what matters most, then decide whether to request repairs, credits, pricing changes, or proceed as-is based on risk and value.
