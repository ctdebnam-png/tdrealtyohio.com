A Different Kind of Brokerage

TD Realty Ohio is a Central Ohio real estate brokerage that operates on a simple premise: the traditional commission model doesn't make sense anymore for a lot of people.

The 3% listing commission became standard when agents had to advertise in newspapers, host open houses to attract buyers, and spend significant time and money marketing each listing. Today, MLS syndication pushes listings to Zillow, Realtor.com, and every major real estate website automatically. The marketing costs that justified 3% have largely disappeared, but the fees haven't.

TD Realty Ohio charges less because the business model is more efficient. Lower overhead, fewer layers of management, no franchise fees going to a corporate headquarters. Those savings go directly to clients and agents.

For Sellers

Homeowners selling through TD Realty Ohio pay 1% listing commission when they sell and buy with the brokerage, or 2% for sell-only transactions. Every listing includes a free pre-listing inspection, a $400–500 value (estimate, Feb 2026) that helps sellers price accurately and avoid surprises during buyer inspections. Full MLS exposure, professional photography, offer review and counteroffer drafting, and contract management through closing are standard.

For First-Time Buyers

First-time homebuyers working with TD Realty Ohio receive 1% of the purchase price back at closing. On a $300,000 home, that's $3,000 that can go toward closing costs, buying down the interest rate, or covering move-in expenses. Full buyer representation is included.

Why TD Realty Ohio Was Founded

Broker/Owner Travis Debnam has been selling homes in Central Ohio since 2017, closing 48 transactions across the metro area with a 5.0 rating on Zillow. In 2023, he earned his broker’s license and founded TD Realty Ohio after seeing the same problem repeatedly: homeowners were paying 3% listing commissions for services that no longer cost 3% to deliver.

The internet changed how homes are marketed. MLS syndication, online photography tools, and digital transaction management have dramatically reduced the cost of selling a home. But commission rates stayed where they were 30 years ago. Too many sellers, especially first-time sellers and people on tight budgets, were handing over thousands of dollars they didn't need to spend.

TD Realty Ohio launched in 2023 with a straightforward goal: provide MLS exposure, professional photography, showing coordination, pricing analysis, and contract-to-close management at a rate that reflects what it actually costs. No franchise fees, no corporate overhead, no layers of management taking a cut.

A Growing Team

What started as a one-broker operation has grown into a brokerage that is actively expanding its team of licensed agents across Central Ohio. Every agent at TD Realty Ohio shares the same philosophy: deliver full-service representation without the inflated commission. Our team combines local market knowledge with modern tools to serve clients across the Columbus metro.

Based in Westerville and serving the Columbus metro, our agents help clients selling starter homes in Reynoldsburg, buying first places in Clintonville, or upgrading in Dublin keep more of their equity.

How Low Commission Works

A common question: how can TD Realty Ohio offer 1-2% commissions while others charge 3%?

Technology

Modern tools and systems streamline paperwork, communication, and marketing, reducing overhead without reducing service quality.

Efficiency

Focused operations and direct broker involvement on every transaction mean less overhead and more value passed to clients.

Volume

Lower rates attract more clients. More transactions at lower margins can be more sustainable than fewer transactions at higher margins.

The real estate industry has traditionally operated on commission structures established decades ago. Technology and modern business practices make it possible to deliver the same service for less. TD Realty Ohio passes those savings to clients.
