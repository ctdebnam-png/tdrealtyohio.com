About the Brokerage

TD Realty Ohio is a Central Ohio residential brokerage.

The brokerage serves buyers and sellers across the Columbus region, and also supports independent agents who want a clear operating model. The approach is direct and practical: local market context, strong negotiation support, and organized contract-to-close execution.

What TD Realty Ohio is

A local residential brokerage

TD Realty Ohio focuses on residential transactions in Central Ohio, including purchase, sale, and selected leasing support.

A transaction-focused operation

Work centers on planning, negotiations, contract details, timeline management, and communication across all parties.

Who we serve and where we operate

Buyers

Home buyers who need clear guidance from financing prep through offer strategy and closing milestones.

Sellers

Home sellers who want disciplined pricing, launch execution, and confident offer review support.

Central Ohio markets

Primary coverage includes Columbus and surrounding communities throughout the region.

Operating approach

How transactions are handled

  • Clear planning at the start of each engagement.
  • Regular communication through major milestones.
  • Structured coordination with lenders, title, inspectors, and other parties.
  • Practical guidance during negotiation and contract decisions.
