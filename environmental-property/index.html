Commercial property with a past

Selling a gas station, dry cleaner, auto shop or older industrial site?

These properties scare off a lot of buyers, lenders and brokers. They don't scare us. Tell us about yours and we'll send a free broker opinion of value, plus a plain-English read on how its history affects price, buyers and financing.

Get my free opinion of value

Properties we help sell

  • Gas stations and former gas stations, open or closed tank files.
  • Dry cleaners and the buildings that used to house one.
  • Auto repair, body shops, truck terminals and small industrial.
  • Sites with a past release, a state cleanup letter or an old report in the file.

What you'll get

  • A broker opinion of value based on recent sales of similar property.
  • What a buyer's lender will likely ask for, and how long it tends to take.
  • State programs and grants that can help a deal like yours close.

No cost and no obligation. You'll have it within 3 business days.

Why owners call us

Our broker, Christopher Travis Debnam, is also a geologist with years of work on property investigations and due diligence for buyers and lenders. He reads the tank file, the cleanup letter or the old report with you, explains what it means in dollars, and helps you plan the sale around it instead of hoping it doesn't come up.

To keep things clean: TD Realty Ohio is a real estate brokerage and doesn't perform environmental work. If we list your property, we don't prepare or sign its environmental report. The buyer or their lender orders that from an independent firm with no tie to us.
