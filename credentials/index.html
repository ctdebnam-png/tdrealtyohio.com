Commission Disclosure

Commission rates are negotiable and are not set by law. TD Realty Ohio's standard listing commission rates are:

  • 1% listing commission when the seller also purchases their next home through TD Realty Ohio (sell + buy).
  • 2% listing commission for sell-only transactions.

Buyer agent compensation is negotiated separately and is not included in the listing commission rates above. All rates are subject to the terms of a signed listing agreement. Contact us to confirm current pricing for your situation.

Advertising Disclosure

All savings examples presented on this website are estimates based on a comparison to a traditional 3% listing fee. Actual savings vary by transaction, sale price, and the terms of your listing agreement.
