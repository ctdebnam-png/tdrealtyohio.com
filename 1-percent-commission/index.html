Why 1% Commission Makes Sense in Central Ohio's Market

Central Ohio home prices vary significantly by community. In higher-priced areas like Dublin, Powell, and Upper Arlington where medians exceed $500,000, traditional 3% listing commissions cost sellers $15,000 or more on the listing side alone.

At those prices, the difference between 1% and 3% is significant. A seller in Westerville with a $400,000 home saves $8,000. In New Albany, where the median is $650,000, the savings jumps to $13,000. That money can go toward your next home's down payment, moving costs, or simply staying in your pocket.

Meanwhile, the services you receive are identical. Your home gets the same MLS exposure, the same syndication to Zillow, Realtor.com, and Redfin, the same professional photography, and the same offer review and counteroffer support. The only difference is the fee structure, and where the savings end up.

How Low Commission Differs from Discount or Flat-Fee Services

It's important to understand the difference between a 1% full-service listing and flat-fee MLS services or "discount" brokerages that cut corners. With flat-fee MLS, you typically pay $300-$500 to get your home listed, but you handle everything else yourself: photography, showings, negotiations, and paperwork. If something goes wrong during the contract-to-close phase, you're on your own.

TD Realty Ohio's 1% model is fundamentally different. You get a dedicated, licensed agent managing every aspect of your sale. The lower commission comes from operational efficiency: lower overhead, technology-driven workflows, and a lean business model, not from reducing your level of service. To learn more, see our detailed comparison of real estate options.

The Pre-Listing Inspection Advantage

One thing that sets TD Realty Ohio apart from both traditional and discount agents is the free pre-listing home inspection included with every listing. Valued at $400-$500, this inspection identifies issues before your home hits the market. Having a seller-provided inspection report builds trust with buyers and can reduce fall-through rates by eliminating surprises during due diligence.

Knowing your home's condition upfront also helps with accurate pricing. Overpriced homes sit on the market, while accurately priced homes attract competitive offers quickly. The pre-listing inspection gives you the data to price right from day one.

Frequently Asked Questions About 1% Commission

How can you offer 1% commission when other agents charge 3%?
We've built an efficient brokerage with lower overhead. No fancy office, no bloated staff. We use technology to streamline workflows and pass those savings directly to sellers while providing full-service representation. Many traditional brokerages spend heavily on office space, administrative staff, and brand fees that get passed to sellers through higher commissions.
What's included with the 1% listing fee?
Everything a traditional agent provides: MLS listing, professional photography, marketing and syndication to Zillow/Realtor.com/Redfin, showing coordination, contract negotiation, and support through closing. Plus a free pre-listing home inspection valued at $400-$500 that most traditional agents don't include.
Who qualifies for the 1% rate?
Sellers who are also buying their next home with TD Realty Ohio qualify for 1% listing commission. Sell-only clients pay 2%, which is still significantly less than the traditional 3%. Both rates include the same full-service representation.
Is there a catch or hidden fees?
No hidden fees. The 1% or 2% is the full listing commission. Buyer agent commission is separate and negotiated based on your market and situation. There are no transaction fees, administrative fees, or surprise charges at closing.
How does buyer agent commission work with 1% listing?
The 1% (or 2%) is your listing fee only. Buyer agent compensation is negotiated separately. We'll discuss competitive buyer agent commission options based on current market conditions in your area. This structure is standard across all real estate transactions regardless of the listing commission rate.
Will a home get less marketing exposure at 1%?
No. Your home gets the same MLS listing that every other agent's listing receives. MLS automatically syndicates to Zillow, Realtor.com, Redfin, Trulia, and hundreds of other sites. Professional photos are included. The marketing reach is identical to any 3% listing.
What areas do you serve?
We serve all of Central Ohio including Columbus, Westerville, Dublin, Powell, Delaware, Gahanna, New Albany, Hilliard, Grove City, Pickerington, Reynoldsburg, Upper Arlington, Worthington, and surrounding communities.
Can I still negotiate the sale price effectively with a 1% agent?
Absolutely. Commission rate has no bearing on negotiation skill. Our broker has the same market knowledge, comparable sales data, and negotiation training as any traditional agent. In fact, the pre-listing inspection we provide often strengthens your negotiating position by eliminating surprises that could derail deals or lead to last-minute price reductions.
How does 1% commission compare to FSBO or flat-fee MLS?
FSBO (For Sale By Owner) and flat-fee MLS services can save on listing commission, but you handle everything yourself: pricing, photography, showings, negotiations, contracts, and closing coordination. According to NAR's 2024 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers, the typical FSBO home sold for less than agent-assisted sales. Our 1% commission gives you professional representation at a fraction of the traditional cost. Learn more in our comparison of real estate options.
