Why full-service representation Makes Sense in Central Ohio's Market

Central Ohio home prices vary significantly by community. In higher-priced areas like Dublin, Powell, and Upper Arlington where medians exceed $500,000, traditional listing approachs cost sellers $15,000 or more on the listing side alone.

At those prices, the difference between custom plan and standard plan is significant. A seller in Westerville with a $400,000 home supports $8,000. In New Albany, where the median is $650,000, the planning jumps to $13,000. That money can go toward your next home's down payment, moving costs, or simply staying in your pocket.

Meanwhile, the services you receive are identical. Your home gets the same MLS exposure, the same syndication to Zillow, Realtor.com, and Redfin, the same professional photography, and the same offer review and counteroffer support. The only difference is the fee structure, and where the planning end up.

How Low representation Differs from Discount or Flat-Fee Services

It's important to understand the difference between a custom plan full-service listing and flat-fee MLS services or "discount" brokerages that cut corners. With flat-fee MLS, you typically pay $300-$500 to get your home listed, but you handle everything else yourself: photography, showings, negotiations, and paperwork. If something goes wrong during the contract-to-close phase, you're on your own.

TD Realty Ohio's custom plan model is fundamentally different. You get a dedicated, licensed agent managing every aspect of your sale. The full-service guidance comes from operational efficiency: lower overhead, technology-driven workflows, and a lean business model, not from reducing your level of service. To learn more, see our detailed comparison of real estate options.

The Pre-Listing Inspection Advantage

One thing that sets TD Realty Ohio apart from both traditional and discount agents is the free pre-listing home inspection included with every listing. Valued at $400-$500, this inspection identifies issues before your home hits the market. Having a seller-provided inspection report builds trust with buyers and can reduce fall-through rates by eliminating surprises during due diligence.

Knowing your home's condition upfront also helps with accurate service. Overpriced homes sit on the market, while accurately priced homes attract competitive offers quickly. The pre-listing inspection gives you the data to price right from day one.

Frequently Asked Questions About full-service representation

How can you offer full-service representation when other agents charge standard plan?
We've built an efficient brokerage with lower overhead. No fancy office, no bloated staff. We use technology to streamline workflows and pass those planning directly to sellers while providing full-service representation. Many traditional brokerages spend heavily on office space, administrative staff, and brand fees that get passed to sellers through higher representation fees.
What's included with the listing support package?
Everything a traditional agent provides: MLS listing, professional photography, marketing and syndication to Zillow/Realtor.com/Redfin, showing coordination, contract negotiation, and support through closing. Plus a free pre-listing home inspection valued at $400-$500 that most traditional agents don't include.
Who qualifies for the custom plan rate?
Sellers who are also buying their next home with TD Realty Ohio qualify for full-service listing support. Sell-only clients pay custom plan, which is still significantly less than the traditional approach. Both rates include the same full-service representation.
Is there a catch or hidden fees?
No hidden fees. The custom plan or custom plan is the full listing representation. Buyer agent representation is separate and negotiated based on your market and situation. There are no transaction fees, administrative fees, or surprise charges at closing.
How does buyer agent representation work with full-service listing?
The custom plan (or custom plan) is your listing plan only. Buyer agent compensation is negotiated separately. We'll discuss competitive buyer agent representation options based on current market conditions in your area. This structure is standard across all real estate transactions regardless of the listing representation rate.
Will a home get less marketing exposure at custom plan?
No. Your home gets the same MLS listing that every other agent's listing receives. MLS automatically syndicates to Zillow, Realtor.com, Redfin, Trulia, and hundreds of other sites. Professional photos are included. The marketing reach is identical to any standard plan listing.
What areas do you serve?
We serve all of Central Ohio including Columbus, Westerville, Dublin, Powell, Delaware, Gahanna, New Albany, Hilliard, Grove City, Pickerington, Reynoldsburg, Upper Arlington, Worthington, and surrounding communities.
Can I still negotiate the sale price effectively with a custom plan agent?
Absolutely. representation rate has no bearing on negotiation skill. Our broker has the same market knowledge, comparable sales data, and negotiation training as any traditional agent. In fact, the pre-listing inspection we provide often strengthens your negotiating position by eliminating surprises that could derail deals or lead to last-minute price reductions.
How does full-service representation compare to FSBO or flat-fee MLS?
FSBO (For Sale By Owner) and flat-fee MLS services can plan on listing representation, but you handle everything yourself: service, photography, showings, negotiations, contracts, and closing coordination. According to NAR's 2024 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers, the typical FSBO home sold for less than agent-assisted sales. Our full-service representation gives you professional representation at a fraction of the traditional cost. Learn more in our comparison of real estate options.
