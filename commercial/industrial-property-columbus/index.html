Commercial guide

Older industrial and flex property in Franklin and Delaware counties

Small and older industrial buildings around Columbus are in demand from owner-users, local investors and developers. They also carry more questions than a new warehouse. We help owners sell them and buyers find them.

Tell us about your property

Who is buying

  • Owner-users: contractors, distributors and small manufacturers who want to stop leasing.
  • Local investors who buy older buildings, update them and lease them as flex space.
  • Developers who want the land for storage, flex or a different use altogether.

County records show 100 sales of industrial and warehouse property in Franklin and Delaware counties over the past year, at a median price of $3,018,360. Details are in the commercial market report.

What drives value

  • Clear height, dock doors and drive-in doors.
  • Power, sprinklers and office share.
  • Zoning and outdoor storage rights.
  • Land size and room to expand.
  • Access to I-70, I-71, I-270 and the rail lines.

What slows a deal down

  • Survey and title problems on older parcels.
  • A use the current zoning no longer allows.
  • Roof, HVAC and paving at the end of their life.
  • Past uses the buyer's lender will ask about, such as plating, machining or fuel storage.

How we help

  • Selling: a broker opinion of value from recent sales, marketing to owner-users and investors, and a plan for the lender's questions before they come up.
  • Buying: help finding buildings that fit your use, offer terms with time for your homework, and coordination through closing.

Our broker, Christopher Travis Debnam, is also a geologist, which helps on buildings with a long history of use. Read more about our commercial real estate services, or see how we sell gas stations and former fuel sites.

To keep things clean: TD Realty Ohio is a real estate brokerage and doesn't perform environmental work. When we broker a property, we don't prepare or sign its environmental report. The buyer or their lender orders that from an independent firm with no tie to us.
