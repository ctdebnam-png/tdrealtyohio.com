How these numbers were built

From Franklin and Delaware county auditor transfer records for parcels classed as commercial, industrial or apartments, with a recorded sale price. A sale that covered several parcels is counted once. Not every recorded transfer is an open-market sale, so treat medians as a guide, not an appraisal.
