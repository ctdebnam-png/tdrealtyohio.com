Who We Are

A residential brokerage built for real Central Ohio transactions.

TD Realty Ohio is a residential brokerage serving buyers, sellers, and landlords in Central Ohio. We focus on practical execution: pricing, negotiations, contract details, timelines, and communication that helps people make confident decisions.

Whether you're moving locally, relocating inside the region, or evaluating your next listing strategy, the goal is simple—keep the process clear, proactive, and professionally managed.

Core coverage and approach

  • Residential buying and selling representation.
  • Service across Columbus-area communities and surrounding markets.
  • Clear offer and risk guidance during key decisions.
  • Straightforward updates at each major milestone.
