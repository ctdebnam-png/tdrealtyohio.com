Lower Listing Fee

Your listing fee is 1% if you also buy with us, or 2% if you're selling only. On a $300,000 sale, you save $3,000–$6,000 compared to a 3% listing fee. Buyer agent compensation is separate and negotiated per transaction.

Full-Service Representation

You get MLS listing, professional photos, syndication to Zillow/Realtor.com/Redfin, showing coordination, offer review and counteroffer drafting, and contract management through closing. We handle everything a traditional agent does.

Local, Direct Communication

Based in Westerville and licensed in Ohio. You work directly with your agent from listing to closing. No call centers, no hand-offs, no out-of-state teams.
