How these numbers were built

From Franklin County auditor transfer records for single-family homes and condos with a Westerville mailing address, counting only sales the auditor marks as valid, one parcel per sale, at $50,000 or more. Homes on the Delaware County side of Westerville aren't included yet. These are recorded sales, not an appraisal. For a value on your home, use the form below or see Westerville home values.
