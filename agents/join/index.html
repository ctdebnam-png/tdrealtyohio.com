The model that matches your stance

Keep your business, buy a back office.

Listing heavy agents keep 100 percent on listings, buyer heavy agents get 90/10, and everyone gets brokerage level E and O plus Dotloop, with a hard annual cap.

How pricing works

Listings

100% on listings

Less a $495 to $995 file fee per closed listing side

Buyers

90/10 on buyer sides

Plus the same $495 to $995 file fee per closed buyer side

Cap

Annual cap on the 10%

$8,000 to $12,000, then effectively 95/5 or 100% less file fee for rest of year

This is a back office product. No required meetings. No bundled leads. No coaching program.

What’s included (what you are actually buying)

  • Dotloop included
  • Brokerage level E and O included
  • Written transaction review turnaround time: Reviewed within one business day after complete submission
  • Escalation path for contract questions: Same day response during business hours
  • Commission disbursement promise: Paid within 24 hours of confirmed funding and receipt of complete file

What’s not included

  • No training
  • No leads
  • No hand holding
  • Agents run their own marketing and client acquisition
  • No required meetings

Who this is for

Experienced agents who want maximum take home and already have systems. This model is built for agents who close business consistently and want low overhead without changing how they generate clients.

Newer agents who already have a mentor or team, but need clean compliance and low friction. If your deal flow and training are already handled elsewhere, this structure gives you operations coverage without extra programs.
