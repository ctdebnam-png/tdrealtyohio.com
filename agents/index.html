Agent FAQ

How is compensation structured?

That is a direct conversation with the broker, based on your production and the kind of business you write. It is not something to evaluate from a web page.

How much broker access do I actually get?

Direct access. You contact the broker when a transaction needs a decision, rather than routing through a team lead or a support queue.

Do I keep control of my own business?

Yes. This is built for agents who generate and manage their own pipeline. Oversight applies to transaction compliance, not to how you run your business.

Can I ask questions before making a move?

Absolutely. Initial conversations are for evaluating fit, expectations, and how the model aligns with your business.

Want to discuss whether this model fits your business?

Start with a direct conversation about your current production, goals, and the type of support you want. Compensation is part of that conversation.
