Agent FAQ

Is the listing-side structure really 100%?

Yes. Listing-side transactions are structured at 100% on the sale side.

How are buy-side deals handled?

Buy-side transactions are handled on a 90/10 split.

How are rental transactions paid?

Rental transactions are paid at 25% of the first month’s lease.

Can I ask questions before making a move?

Absolutely. Initial conversations are for evaluating fit, expectations, and how the model aligns with your business.
